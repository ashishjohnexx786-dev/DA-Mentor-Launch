# 3-Minute Project Walkthrough

## 0:00-0:25 — Decision
What business decision/question did the project support?

## 0:25-0:50 — Data + grain
What sources did you use and what did one row represent?

## 0:50-1:15 — Quality issue
What could have broken the analysis and what did you do?

## 1:15-1:45 — Method
What did you build and why was that method appropriate?

## 1:45-2:10 — Validation
How did you independently verify at least one material result?

## 2:10-2:35 — Findings
Give 2-3 findings with comparison and magnitude.

## 2:35-2:50 — Recommendation
What should happen next and how would success be measured?

## 2:50-3:00 — Limitation
What can the data/design not prove?
