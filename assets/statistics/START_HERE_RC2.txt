STATISTICAL INTERPRETATION BOUNDARY
RC2 preserves the canonical beginner decision rule while tightening language. A p-value is not P(H0 true); fail-to-reject is not proof of no effect; CI precision does not prove unbiased sampling; statistical significance is not business importance; and a point estimate crossing an MWE is not the same as the entire uncertainty interval clearing that MWE.
