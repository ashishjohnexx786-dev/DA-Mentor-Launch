INTERPRETATION RULES
Large n improves precision but does not repair systematic sampling bias.
A p-value is not the probability that H0 is true.
Failing to reject H0 does not prove there is no effect.
Statistical significance is not the same as practical/business significance.
A narrow confidence interval does not prove the sample is unbiased.
Correlation alone does not establish causation.
Experiment decisions combine design quality, effect size, uncertainty/statistical evidence, business threshold and guardrails.
